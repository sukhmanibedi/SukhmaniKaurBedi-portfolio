MY METHODOLOGY

Design Process

How I take messy enterprise problems and make them clear, actionable, and scalable.

Systems Design & Workflow Mapping
01

Map the system, not the screen

Enterprise users operate inside complex policy rules and multi-step workflows. Before designing interfaces, I chart end-to-end user journeys, off-system workarounds, and legacy technology dependencies.

02

Find where complexity is hiding

Complexity accumulates silently in manual spreadsheets and email chains. Utilizing methods like IBM Complexity Analysis, I quantify friction points to prioritize high-value redesign targets.

03

Design within real constraints

I build using scalable component libraries (like IBM Carbon) to ensure rapid developer handoff, strict regulatory compliance, and consistent data density across views.

04

Validate before shipping

Enterprise software impacts daily operational output. I conduct rigorous usability testing with domain experts and operators to confirm accuracy before deployment.
