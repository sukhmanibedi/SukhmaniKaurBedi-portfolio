IBM PowerVC Case Study

Redesigning IBM PowerVC to streamline virtualization management for enterprise administrators managing thousands of VMs.

Focus GroupSystem Admins
SectorCloud Management
RoleUX Researcher & Designer
Duration23 months

Context

IBM PowerVC was a mission-critical platform used by enterprise administrators. Its interface complexity directly impacted productivity, training costs, and confidence in managing scale.

Challenge

  • Fragmented workflows for VM creation and management.
  • Inconsistent GUI vs API experiences.
  • Limited bulk operations.
  • Lack of real-time feedback.
  • High training costs due to complexity.

Research

We partnered with 8 enterprise customers, analyzed 140+ NPS comments, and conducted heuristic evaluations across 6 workflows. This triangulation ensured both breadth and depth of insights.

Personas

Alan — System Architect

Needs strategic dashboards, predictive analytics, and consistent UI across IBM tools.

Monica — Cloud Systems Admin

Needs streamlined workflows, robust bulk actions, and clear real-time feedback.

Steve — Technical User

Needs intuitive navigation, prioritization indicators, and contextual in-app guidance.

Design Solutions

Persistent filters, simplified navigation, labeled actions, and scalable host management. These changes reduced VM search time by 40% and lowered onboarding costs by 30%.

From cluttered to streamlined: PowerVC now empowers admins to manage scale with confidence.
