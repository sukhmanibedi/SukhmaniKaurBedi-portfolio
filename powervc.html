IBM PowerVC Case Study

Redesigning IBM PowerVC to streamline virtualization management for system administrators handling thousands of VMs.

Focus GroupSystem Admins
SectorCloud Management
RoleUX Researcher & Designer
Duration23 months

Project Overview

Administrators faced deep navigation hierarchies, cluttered dashboards, and poor contextual guidance. The redesign aimed to simplify workflows, reduce cognitive load, and improve confidence in managing scale.

Problem Statement

  • Fragmented workflows for VM creation and management.
  • Inconsistent GUI vs API experiences.
  • Limited bulk operations.
  • Lack of real-time feedback.
  • High training costs due to complexity.

Research

We partnered with 6–8 customers, ran TechU surveys, and analyzed 140+ NPS comments. A heuristic evaluation flagged critical usability violations across dashboards, VM management, and configuration flows.

Persona

Rahul, a system admin managing 150 hosts. His biggest frustration: scrolling endlessly to find VM filters.

152

RFEs gathered

140

NPS comments analyzed

Heuristic Evaluation

Issues included false affordances, buried status metrics, inconsistent icons, and cluttered host lists. Fixes focused on visibility, consistency, and scalable design patterns.

Design Solutions

Persistent filters, simplified navigation, labeled actions, and scalable host management. These changes reduced VM search time by 40% and lowered training overhead.

From cluttered to streamlined: PowerVC now empowers admins to manage scale with confidence.
