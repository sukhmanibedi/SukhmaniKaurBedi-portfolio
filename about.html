BACKGROUND & PHILOSOPHY

About Me

Enterprise design isn’t about building beautiful products. It’s about understanding the person who has to use that product at 9:00 AM on a Monday, under pressure, with no margin for error—and the business that depends on them getting it right.

Most design problems in complex systems aren’t interface problems. They’re the result of workflows that were never designed, business logic that was never questioned, and friction that accumulated quietly. Finding that friction before it reaches the user is where I spend my time.

I’m Sukhmani Kaur Bedi. As a Senior UX Designer and Researcher at IBM, I design enterprise software for high-stakes client environments: IBM PowerVC, Atradius, and European Central Bank core platforms. In this space, empathy alone isn’t enough; you have to hold the user’s reality and the business's constraints in the same frame, and design the experience that makes both possible.
